# Lantern Delve

Lantern Delve turns homework time into fuel for an adventure.

- **Focus to earn Light.** Start a focus session and every focused minute earns Light. Wick, a little candle,
  studies with you in a small window, and social media, streaming and games get a warning, then start costing Light.
- **Homework in tiny steps.** Add an assignment (attach the sheet or rubric if you like) and it gets split into
  small, doable steps. Checking them off earns bonus Light and treasure chests.
- **Spend it on the adventure.** Light powers Wick's lantern in a paper-craft world: explore fields, caves and
  towns, fight with timed attacks, find secrets, team up with partners and play carnival minigames on your breaks.
- **Come back tomorrow.** Daily quests and a day streak reward showing up.

- ✅ Focus sessions with Wick, the study buddy, plus a distraction blocker and Claude focus checks (Windows app)
- ✅ Homework split into tiny steps, with file attachments and a Completed folder
- ✅ Real due dates (or none): assignments are grouped by when they're due, overdue first, with a Coming up list on the Today tab
- ✅ A start menu when the game opens
- ✅ Light, daily quests, streaks and treasure chests
- ✅ The whole story: the Prologue, Chapters 1 to 4 and the ending
- ✅ A Bonus Chapter after the ending: the Unfinished Margin, a pencil-sketch world past the edge of the map, with a new partner (Doodle the crayon), new enemies and two new bosses
- ✅ Big open fields, side quests, carnival minigames and Lantern upgrades
- ✅ Battles with timed attacks, partners and badges (fire enemies now spit embers, flare up and erupt lava geysers)
- ✅ An original soundtrack
- ✅ Browser and Windows versions that keep themselves up to date
- ✅ More story scenes along the way (and Count Dusk scheming between chapters)
- ✅ Play-test fixes: clearer exits and doorways, hazards in every gap, shadows that show depth, walls that stay put, and the Prologue ending at Mothwick's lookout
- ✅ Smoother doors: walk in with your partner, step out the other side (no more popping or landing on rooftops)
- ✅ A detailed, zoomable map of every place (TAB), with houses, trees, water, doors, chests, folks and enemies on it
- ✅ A new minimap in the corner: a close-up picture map that follows you, with Wick's face pointing the way, a gold pin on where to go next, and a side view in the long caves (click it for the big map)
- ✅ Maps that fill in as you explore: new places start as blank paper, and the TAB map shows how much of each place you've explored
- ⬜ Play-testing the whole story (Chapters 3 and 4, the ending and the Bonus Chapter haven't been played through yet)
- ⬜ Fixing what play-testing turns up
- ⬜ A balance pass: difficulty, prices and Light costs

## Download

Latest version: **2026.11.05.3**

- [Play in your browser](https://github.com/GabeisRandom/lantern-delve/raw/main/Lantern%20Delve%20-%20play%20in%20your%20browser.html): one file, nothing to install. Save it, then open it in Chrome or Edge.
  (A web page can't see your other windows, so focus sessions there run on the honor system, with check-ins.)
- [Windows app](https://github.com/GabeisRandom/lantern-delve/raw/main/Lantern%20Delve%20-%20Windows%20app.zip): the full thing, distraction blocker included. Unzip it and double-click
  "Start Lantern Delve" (needs Python 3).

Both keep themselves up to date, and only install versions signed with the maker's key.
